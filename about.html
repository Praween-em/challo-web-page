About Challo

Building a smarter, safer, and more compassionate transit network for everyone.

Who We Are

A Ride Platform Built for the People

Challo was conceived with a clear vision: everyday urban transportation should not be a daily headache of price surges, sudden cancellations, or unviable margins for hard-working drivers.

We believe mobility is an essential lifeline that powers local economies, facilitates education, enables healthcare access, and connects communities. By combining lightweight mobile technology with local operational expertise, Challo delivers a seamless ride-hailing experience tailored specifically to the regional dynamics of Indian cities.

From daily two-wheeler commuters to families traveling across town or patients requiring immediate hospital transport, our platform ensures reliable vehicles are accessible with just a few taps.

Purpose & Direction

What Drives Challo Forward

🎯

Our Mission

To provide accessible, affordable, and safe mobility solutions that empower passengers with prompt transit and driver partners with dignity, equitable earnings, and sustainable growth.

🌍

Our Vision

To become the most dependable and commuter-loved transportation network across Tier-1, Tier-2, and regional urban centres, bridging the gap between riders and drivers.

🤝

Our Values

Integrity, total fare transparency, zero exploitation of drivers, and unwavering commitment to passenger safety on every single kilometer traveled.

Leadership

Guided by Community Commitment

Under the strategic stewardship of Dr. B. Anil Kumar (Chairman and Chief Editor), Challo is positioned not merely as a tech application, but as a community mobility initiative.

Our leadership advocates strongly for driver partner welfare, providing accident coverage assurances, community help desks, transparent fee frameworks, and emergency medical vehicle integration for citywide relief.

Safety Guarantee

Safety is Not an Afterthought. It is Our Foundation.

Every trip on Challo is monitored in real time with end-to-end GPS technology, verified captain profiles, one-tap emergency SOS alarms, and dedicated safety response protocols to provide complete peace of mind to riders and their families.
