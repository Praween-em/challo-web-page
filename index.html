Introducing Challo

Redefining Everyday Mobility in Your City

Challo is an indigenous ride-booking technology platform built to solve the everyday commuting challenges of citizens across Telangana. Whether you are navigating peak traffic hours on a bike taxi, taking an auto to the railway station, booking a family cab, or requiring emergency ambulance transport — Challo connects you to reliable drivers in real-time.

With a driver-first philosophy offering an honest commission model and passenger-centric safety protocols, Challo bridges convenience, speed, and affordability.
