Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

Data Privacy: This policy describes how Challo collects, uses, and safeguards information gathered when you download, install, or use our mobile applications and services.

1. Introduction

At Challo, we prioritize the privacy and security of our passengers and ride partners. This document outlines our data governance policies and the explicit rights you hold regarding your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only such information as is strictly necessary to deliver efficient, safe, and transparent ride-hailing services:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Name, mobile phone number, and optional email address collected during account registration.
  • Location Information: Precise and approximate geographic location collected through GPS, Wi-Fi, and cellular signals when the app is active in the foreground or background (for ongoing ride navigation and safety tracking).
  • Ride & Transactional Data: Pickup and drop-off coordinates, route timestamps, vehicle category selected, total distance, fare calculations, and payment status.
  • Device & Technical Data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and crash reports.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is processed to:

  • Facilitate instant driver matching and accurate route navigation.
  • Calculate transparent fares based on real-time road distances.
  • Ensure passenger and driver safety through continuous live trip monitoring and emergency SOS functionality.
  • Send booking confirmations, OTP verification codes, digital trip receipts, and critical service notifications.
  • Prevent fraudulent activities, duplicate accounts, and unauthorized platform access.
  • Resolve customer support tickets, fare disputes, and grievances.

4. Precise Location Permissions

Location data is the core enabler of ride-hailing functionality:

  • Foreground Location: Used to identify your current pickup address and display nearby available Captains.
  • Background Location (During Active Trip): For driver partners and passengers, background location tracks the vehicle along the designated route to ensure adherence to safety protocols until trip completion.
  • You may revoke location permissions through device settings at any time; however, real-time ride booking cannot function without location access.

5. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Information is shared strictly under the following operational circumstances:

  • With Ride Partners (Captains): Passenger first name, pickup location, and drop-off coordinates are shared for trip execution.
  • With Passengers: Captain registered name, vehicle model, vehicle registration number, and contact option for pickup coordination.
  • Emergency Services: Direct patient location and contact numbers are provided to authorized medical transport (Ambulance) drivers to ensure immediate response without delay.
  • Law Enforcement: If an in-app SOS alert is triggered or where legally mandated by judicial orders or regulatory authorities under Indian law.

6. Data Security Measures

We enforce robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures including SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit across mobile devices and servers.

7. User Rights & Account Deletion

Users possess the right to access and review profile details inside the app, and request permanent deactivation of accounts and associated personal data by writing to ridechallo@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".

8. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

For questions, feedback, or grievance redressal concerning this Privacy Policy, please contact our support desk: ridechallo@gmail.com.
