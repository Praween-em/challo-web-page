2. Description of the Challo Platform

Challo operates an intermediary technology platform connecting independent passenger users looking for transportation ("Passengers") with registered, third-party independent vehicle operators and fleet owners ("Ride Partners" or "Captains"). The transportation categories enabled on the platform include:

  • Bike Taxi: Two-wheeler motorized point-to-point passenger transport.
  • Auto Taxi: Three-wheeler passenger transport operated as per applicable regional meter/guideline tariffs.
  • Car Taxi: Four-wheeler private cabs (Hatchback, Sedan, SUV).
  • Ambulance Service: On-demand non-emergency and emergency medical passenger transfer facilitation.

3. User Eligibility & Account Registration

To create an account and book rides on Challo, you must be at least 18 years of age or possess legal contracting capacity under Indian law. You agree to provide accurate and verifiable phone registration details.

4. Ride Booking & Cancellations

A booking is confirmed when an assigned Captain accepts the trip request and an OTP is generated. Passengers may cancel requests before or after assignment. If cancellation occurs after the Captain has arrived at the pickup location, a reasonable cancellation fee may apply to compensate the Captain's fuel and time.

5. Fare Estimates & Payments

Challo provides upfront fare estimates based on calculated distance, route duration, vehicle type, and prevailing base tariffs. Users can pay through approved electronic channels (UPI, Debit/Credit Card, Mobile Wallets) or Cash directly to the Captain upon ride completion.
